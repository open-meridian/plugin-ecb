## The ECB's terms

What this plugin's builders noted, to check against the ECB's own words,
which can change. Not legal advice.

- The ECB's website allows free use of the information obtained from it,
  provided it is reproduced accurately and the ECB is cited as the source;
  where the information is modified, that must be stated; where it is
  included in something sold, buyers must be told it is free from the ECB
  ([copyright and disclaimer](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/home/disclaimer/html/index.en.html)).
- The reference rates are published for information purposes only, and the
  ECB discourages using them for transactions
  ([euro foreign exchange reference rates](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/index.en.html)).
- The ECB accepts no liability for decisions made in reliance on the rates.
- €STR: the ECB says it does not charge for the €STR or license its use;
  its website's terms above apply to what is read from it
  ([euro short-term rate](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/financial_markets_and_interest_rates/euro_short-term_rate/html/index.en.html)).
- No key and no account: the plugin reads the ECB's public Data Portal.
